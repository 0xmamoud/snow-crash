From the level00 account, the goal was to find the password for the flag00 account.

I started by searching for files owned by the flag00 user:

find / -user flag00 2>/dev/null

Interesting results:

/usr/sbin/john
/rofs/usr/sbin/john

The /usr/sbin/john file contained:

cdiiddwpgswtgt

This string was not the password directly. It was encrypted with a Caesar/ROT cipher.
By testing the shifts with https://cryptii.com/pipes/caesar-cipher/, shift 15 gives:

nottoohardhere

I used this password to log into the flag00 account:

su flag00

Then I ran:

getflag

Token obtained to access the next level:

x24ti5gi3x0ol2eh4esiuxias
